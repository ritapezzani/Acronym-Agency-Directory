New CEM Training Guide: Milestone Tracker & Agency Success

A comprehensive onboarding playbook for Customer Engagement Managers to navigate role expectations, team structure, tracking tools, and agency transitions.

Welcome to the team. As a Customer Engagement Manager (CEM), you are the critical bridge between your assigned agencies and the GSA PMO. This guide synthesizes the resources available to help you navigate your new role, master the Milestone Tracker, and locate necessary project documentation.

1. CEM Roles & Objectives

Your primary objective is to ensure your assigned agency’s transition to the GO.gov platform is successful.

  • The Bridge: You act as the primary point of contact, ensuring consistent communication between the GSA PMO and your agency stakeholders.
  • The Data Custodian: The data you provide in the Milestone Tracker directly informs Agency Transition Scorecards, which guide GSA leadership decisions.
  • Operational Support: You facilitate training, guide agencies through configuration, and manage risk by keeping documentation accurate and current.

2. Team Ecosystem & Contracting Structure

Understanding team boundaries and contractor dynamics ensures smooth operational coordination:

  • Prime Contractor: Censeo is the prime contractor responsible for BPA and Call Order (CO) management, delivery quality, cost, schedule, and risk.
  • Subcontractors: Guidehouse and Genesis provide T&E expertise supporting governance, communications, transition, and ETS history. Direct all subcontractor team member concerns to Kisha and Baylee, not GSA.
  • TMC Support: GoldSpring supports Travel Management Company (TMC) transitions and operates independently (they are not a Censeo subcontractor).

3. Key Tools & Communication Channels

You will utilize several specific tools and channels to manage your workflow:

  • Milestone Tracker: Your "live status board" for tracking agency progress against go-live timelines.
  • Connect.gov & Resource Hub: The central and definitive repository where all customers and CEMs are directed to find official documentation, training materials, and technical specifications.
  • Project Team Meetings (PTM): Collaborative syncs held regularly to review updates and remove operational blockers.
  • Google Spaces Norms: Active spaces include Censeo Team, GSA (Customer), IBM (MSP), and MSR Review (primarily for Censeo internal prep before Monthly Status Reports are delivered to GSA).

4. Essential Documentation & Reading

When you need to find official resources, Connect.gov is your single source of truth. Strongly recommended review priorities include:

  • FOC-B project plan template and Technology Solution Final
  • 2026-05-21 ConcurGov Crosswalk and E2 Crosswalk
  • GO.gov glossary of terms as of January 2025
  • Security documentation (requires an NDA; email CEM leads and cc gogov@gsa.gov for access)

5. Next Steps & Administrative Onboarding

  • Coffee Chats: Review the "GO.gov Introduction Team Coffee Chats" tracker to schedule 30-minute introductions with teammates to discuss backgrounds, roles, and key insights.
  • Time Tracking Reminder: Code internal training and onboarding hours to OH-PD, and client/project tasks to your project codes (e.g., GSA) to fulfill your daily hours.

Milestone Tracker: Guidelines & Task Index

Framework for tracking, editing rules, logic alerts, life-cycle breakdown, roadblocks matrix, escalation flow, and searchable reference list of individual tasks.

Mastering the Milestone Tracker

The Milestone Tracker is the primary tool for agency visibility. Below is the framework for managing it.

Rules for Editing

  • Column D (Percentage Complete): Update this only for the lowest-level tasks (e.g., 1.2.3). The system automatically calculates higher-level percentages.
  • Column H (Comments): Use this for context, especially if a task is "Behind" or "Significantly Behind." Always identify roadblocks and your mitigation strategy.

What to Avoid

  • Pre-filled Fields: Do not edit Agency or Implementation Phase cells; these are linked to the Master Tracker.
  • Task Status: Calculated automatically based on time elapsed vs. completion. Manual overrides are not permitted.
  • Dates: Managed centrally by the GSA PMO.

Logic & Alerts

  • Grey Cells: Locked. These rely on a "Dependent Task" (Column J). They unlock automatically when the dependent task hits 100%.
  • Status Alerts: Tasks flagged as "Behind" are based on time-elapsed calculations. Do not panic; use the Comment column to explain the delay and plan to recover.

Phase-by-Phase Lifecycle Roadmap

Breakdown of the overarching lifecycle to help CEMs guide agencies through key transition phases:

  • Phase 1: Pre-Transition Planning: Stakeholder alignment, budget validation, confirming the agency champion, and kicking off As-Is / To-Be gap analyses.
  • Phase 2: Transition Execution: MSP kickoff meetings, data mapping, Financial Management (FM) and Single Sign-On (SSO) integration builds, Pre-SIT testing, and user profile loading.
  • Phase 3: Deployments: Training rollout, final production data loads, TMC setup, and go-live day support.
  • Phase 4: ETS Close-Out: Extracting final historical archives from the legacy system, reconciling Travel Management Company (TMC) accounts, and shutting down old billing lines.

Common Roadblocks & Mitigation Matrix

Quick-reference guide for navigating common agency bottlenecks:

  • Roadblock: Agency security team delayed on ATO/ATU package.
    Mitigation: Escalate early through the GSA security lead, verify CRM matches the FedRAMP package, and promptly request the Interconnection Security Agreement (ISA) template.
  • Roadblock: Financial Management (FM) interface custom mapping failing.
    Mitigation: Reference latest ConcurGov/E2 crosswalks on Connect.gov and schedule an immediate technical sync with the MSP team.
  • Roadblock: Low user engagement or resistance to change.
    Mitigation: Lean on internal Change Champions and deploy targeted training modules based on the needs analysis.

Status Definitions

  • On Track (Green): Meeting all scheduled timelines with no active blockers.
  • Behind (Yellow): Minor delay with a clear recovery plan documented in Column H.
  • Significantly Behind (Red): Major blocker threatening the go-live window requiring GSA PMO intervention.
  • Complete (Blue/Grey): Task fully executed and validated.
